Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UUID Generator Pro handles data when you use the browser-based UUID generation and validation tool.

Client-side UUID generation

UUID Generator Pro generates UUIDs directly in your browser. Random UUIDs are created using browser cryptography, including the Web Cryptography API where supported. Generated UUIDs, validation input, namespaces, names, and export data are not sent to a UUID Generator Pro server for processing.

Local storage

The tool may use your browser's localStorage to remember local preferences and convenience data. Current localStorage keys may include theme for the light or dark display preference, uuidHistory for recent generated UUIDs shown in the history panel, and uuidGeneratorCookieConsent for the cookie notice acknowledgement. This information stays in your browser unless you clear it, change browsers, use a private browsing mode, or your browser removes it.

Server data

UUID Generator Pro is designed as a static, client-side tool. We do not operate account registration, server-side UUID storage, or server-side UUID generation for this site. Standard hosting infrastructure may still process routine technical information, such as IP address, user agent, requested page, and timestamps, to deliver pages and maintain security.

Google AdSense and cookies

This site may display ads through Google AdSense using publisher client ca-pub-6100778423642867. Google and its advertising partners may place or read cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers, and similar technologies as a result of ad serving on this site.

Google may use advertising cookies to serve personalised ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalised ads through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn about interest-based advertising choices at aboutads.info and Your Online Choices.

For a clear explanation of how Google uses data on partner sites, read How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

Your choices

  • You can clear localStorage and browsing data in your browser settings.
  • You can use private browsing modes if you do not want UUID history or theme preferences retained after the session.
  • You can manage ad personalisation through Google, industry opt-out tools, and your browser's cookie settings.

Owner and contact

UUID Generator Pro is maintained by Chameleon Partnership as part of its browser utility sites. For privacy questions about this site, use the contact route provided on the About page.

About the Maintainer

UUID Generator Pro is maintained by a developer tooling team specialising in privacy-first browser utilities for software teams. The tool follows RFC 4122 conventions, uses browser cryptography for random UUIDs, and is reviewed for accessible controls and practical export workflows.
